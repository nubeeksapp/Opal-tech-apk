Privacy Policy for Opal Tech

App Package: com.aistudio.opaltech.kvptzq • Developer: NUBEEKS • Effective Date: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NUBEEKS ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the Opal Tech Android application ("the App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect and Process

  • No Account or Personal Profile Required: Opal Tech does not require you to create an account, log in, or provide your name, address, or payment details.
  • Local Device Data: Your bookmarked/favorite articles, reading preferences (such as dark mode and text size), search history, and offline article cache are stored locally on your device.
  • Advertising (Google AdMob): The App displays banner, native, and interstitial ads provided by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may automatically collect and process device identifiers (including the Android Advertising ID), IP address, coarse location derived from IP, and ad interaction events (impressions and clicks) to serve, cap, and measure advertisements.
  • Push Notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging): If you grant notification permission on Android, Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) generates an anonymous device registration token used solely to deliver tech tip and new article notifications.
  • Content Delivery & View Counts (Supabase): The App connects to our backend database to fetch published tech articles and categories and to record anonymous article view counts.

2. How We Use Information

  • To deliver, synchronize, and display technology articles, app reviews, and digital guides.
  • To preserve your local reading preferences, search history, and saved favorites on your device.
  • To send optional push notifications when you have enabled them.
  • To display and measure advertisements that keep the App free to use.

3. Third-Party Services

We do not sell your personal information. The App uses the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Supabase: https://supabase.com/privacy

4. Data Retention & Your Controls

  • Clear Local Data: You can clear your offline article cache and search history at any time inside the App under Settings → General. Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data.
  • Manage Notifications: You can enable or disable push notifications at any time in Settings → General → Push Notification or in your Android device settings.
  • Manage Ad Personalization: You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID at any time in your device's Settings → Google → Ads.

5. Data Security

All network communication between the App and our backend and third-party service providers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.

6. Children's Privacy

Opal Tech is a general-audience technology news and tips application and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
